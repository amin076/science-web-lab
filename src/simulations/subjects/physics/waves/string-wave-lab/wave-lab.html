Formulas & variable definitions

v = √(F / μ) — speed from tension F (newtons) and linear density μ (kg/m).

v = f λ — wavelength λ (metres), frequency f (Hz).

P = 1 / f — temporal period P in seconds; ω = 2πf and k = 2π/λ.

m = μ L — string mass; here L = 12 m.

y = yL + yR — linear displacement superposition. At fixed ends the reflected pulse inverts; free ends preserve its sign.

Ideal uniform string with negligible dispersion and damping. Speed, mass and tension are coupled; changing speed recalculates tension.

Physics note: The wave obeys v = √(F/μ) = fλ. Adjust tension, mass and wave speed as linked physical variables. A fixed end reverses the reflected displacement; a free end preserves it; an absorbing end lets a wave exit. In Two-sided pulses mode, the blue and gold pulses approach from opposite ends, overlap according to y = yleft + yright, and continue through one another. Reverse the right pulse's polarity to demonstrate destructive interference. Dashed curves show the individual contributions; the solid curve shows their sum.
