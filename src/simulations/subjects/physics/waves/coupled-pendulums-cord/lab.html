Physical model and units

Bob: X=x+L sin θ, Y=y+L cos θ. Both knot and bob inertia enter a common Lagrangian model.

Cable segment tension: F=k max(ℓ−ℓ₀,0). Its weight-dependent sag is solved as a static equilibrium before motion begins.

Gravity: U_g=−(M+m)gy−mgL cos θ (downward y). Cable: U_c=Σ½k max(ℓ−ℓ₀,0)².

The cable is a chain of axial springs, with movable material knots — not sliding rings. Cable tension, inertia and pendulum inertia exchange energy.

Energy ledger

In a free, lossless run, E(t)=E(0). With damping and a periodic external drive: E(t)+Q_diss−W_drive=E(0).

Individual pendulum energies are shown separately from cable energy; the partition is not a claim that each part is conserved.

System & elastic support

Choose the number of bobs and edit the real cable's tension, knot mass, and damping.

Lower EA gives a more compliant elastic cord (and usually stronger coupling). Large EA behaves more like a stiff rope. Each knot is tied to its own fixed material index on the rope. It may move slightly with the rope in both directions; it cannot slide along it. The display multiplier exaggerates only support motion.
