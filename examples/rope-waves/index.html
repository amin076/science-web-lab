Physics & assumptions

Ideal uniform rope; constant tension, no damping, small transverse slopes. The vertical oscillator generates the incident wave. Its matched coupler absorbs returning waves; the rope attachment shows total displacement. A sine source ramps up over one period. Pulse duration is 1/f; wavelength and mean incident power describe the sinusoidal setting. Vertical displacement is magnified for visibility.
